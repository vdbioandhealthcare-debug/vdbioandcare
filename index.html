Leadership Message

From Founder's Desk

"Our vision since founding in 2023 is to build a healthier world through science-backed bio-nutraceuticals."

Welcome to VD Biochem & Healthcare. We established our journey in 2023 with a firm commitment to innovation, quality, and ethical healthcare solutions. Guided by the One Health Approach, we recognize the deep interplay between livestock productivity, plant nutrition, and human wellbeing.

Currently, our organization is heavily focused on expanding our Vet Nutraceuticals portfolio. We supply dairy farmers, livestock practitioners, and pet caretakers with high-potency nutritional supplements engineered to improve yield, immunity, and overall animal health.

Strategic Focus Areas:

Currently dealing in specialized Veterinary Nutraceuticals, chelated minerals, liquid feed supplements, and high-absorption metabolic boluses.
